There are three different telemetry streams, and we should not mix them. Customer observability sends diagnostics to destinations chosen by the customer. It does not automatically give the Dab team access. Existing application name telemetry puts a compact configuration fingerprint into supported database connections. It describes connection pools and configured capabilities. The proposed engine stream would send approved product facts to a separately governed destination. Neither a configuration flag nor an open connection proves that a feature was used. Likewise, process sessions are not customer counts. Keeping these meanings separate prevents misleading reports and accidental forwarding of customer diagnostics.
