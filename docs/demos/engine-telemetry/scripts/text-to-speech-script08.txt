Data X adds a managed product-data layer, not the ability to invent custom properties, which Application Insights already supports. The supplied guide describes classification-based privacy processing, applicable data subject request handling, pipeline completeness and anomaly monitoring, shared Kusto, and optional business metrics. The team still patches its client, maintains classifications, controls volume, and fixes unexpected personal data promptly. Onboarding starts with privacy review and validation in our own Application Insights resource. The guide estimates roughly ten days to Kusto, with substantial variability. That is not a promise for designing and shipping Dab telemetry, or for completing every downstream analytics output.
