Cost depends on frequency, payload size, retained history, and ownership. The document uses illustrative scenarios, not a forecast. At two kibibytes per event, ten thousand active processes with one daily start and hourly summaries produce about fifteen gigabytes of payload over thirty days. That is not the Azure bill: service metadata, billable-size rules, and retained copies can change the calculation. More frequent or larger events increase volume. Compare actual workspace charges with the Data X agreement, including maintenance and any migration overlap. Shared does not mean free, and lower client complexity does not automatically mean lower total cost.
