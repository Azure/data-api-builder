One option is to stage platform adoption. First, establish useful, approved reporting with Application Insights and Log Analytics. Then consider Data X only when a specific governance, shared-analysis, or operational benefit justifies the move. Staying at the first stage is a valid long-term outcome. The benefit is learning from real reporting needs before taking on more coordination. The cost is possible later adaptation of clients, schemas, and dashboards. Existing history does not move automatically, and changing a resource does not update old installations. Any transition needs a bounded overlap, validation, retention, and rollback plan. Privacy approval applies from the start.
