The review should end with decisions, not an automatic commitment to collect everything. First, identify the properties that answer useful questions and agree their names and units. Second, choose a minimal identity and consent model. Third, confirm a supported client and publishing path. Finally, decide whether Azure Monitor is sufficient or which specific Data X capability is needed. A later, separately approved synthetic proof of concept can check field mappings, unwanted enrichment, disabled behavior, and volume. This walkthrough creates no telemetry feature or cloud resources. The full design document contains the detailed catalog, tradeoffs, and sources for the team discussion.
